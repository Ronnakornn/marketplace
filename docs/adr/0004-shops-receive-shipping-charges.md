# Shops receive shipping charges

The business owner selected this rule on 2026-10-04 after comparing Shop,
marketplace, and carrier entitlement. A Shipping Charge collected from a buyer
is owed to the Shop, and the Shop is responsible for paying the carrier.
This assigns the shipping receivable and carrier responsibility to the same party.

## Consequences

Shipping entitlement is separate from merchandise earnings and Platform
Commission. The current seller earning path omits shipping, so this decision
records the target policy and does not claim that settlement already implements it.

Discount funding, commission base and reversal, shipping refundability, carrier
costs, and refund debt recovery remain unresolved. This decision does not infer a
shipping subsidy when a free-shipping coupon reduces the collected charge to zero.

See [settlement-domain-model.md](../settlement-domain-model.md) for the source
evidence and pending policy choices.
