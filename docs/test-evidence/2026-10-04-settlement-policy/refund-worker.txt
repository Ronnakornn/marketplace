Implemented snapshot policy planning and completion separately from legacy cash-allocation validation. New-policy refund plans reserve complete order-item quantities, retain immutable original components, and require trusted shipping causes. Pending plan writes acquire a conditional PENDING row lock before replacement. Repeated completion has no additional accounting effects. Commission reversals use original snapshotted fee and successful actual merchandise reversals. Unrecognized sale rights are reversed without wallet debt. Recognized sale reversals post negative refund_adjustment and positive commission_fee separately.

Paid preship cancellation validates a full-shop item scope and shipment evidence, restores committed inventory exactly once, records RETURN_RESTOCK movements, and cancels sale items, Shop Orders, and shipments atomically. Parent status derives from remaining active shipments; all-shop cancellation refunds the parent and successful payment. Seller-fault and change-of-mind returns do not auto-restock. Seller-fault returns retain incurred marketplace shipping subsidy.

Fixed Prisma 7.9 transaction proxy recursion in return creation by explicitly tracking transaction context. No transaction timeout was raised.
