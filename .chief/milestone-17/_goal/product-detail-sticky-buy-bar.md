# Product Detail Sticky Buy Bar

## Goal

Make the sticky buy bar usable and readable on mobile and desktop, especially around variant/quantity/action states.

## In Scope

- Responsive sticky action bar layout.
- Add to cart and Buy now button sizing, labels, disabled states, and pending state.
- Wishlist and chat seller controls where they fit without crowding primary purchase actions.
- Accessible status text for selected variant, quantity, stock, and errors.

## Out of Scope

- New mini cart surface.
- Checkout page redesign.
- Chat redesign.

## Constraints

- Primary actions must remain tappable on mobile.
- Text must not overflow or obscure adjacent controls.
- Existing buyer shell/cart routes must remain compatible.
