# Buyer Product Listing Card UX

## Goal

Improve product listing and search cards so buyers can scan products faster and make better click/add-to-cart decisions.

## In Scope

- Product cards for listing/search/home surfaces where the existing product card is used.
- Clear image, title, price, rating, sold count, shop/location, stock, and quick-add affordances where data exists.
- Better responsive card density for mobile and desktop.
- Loading, empty, and unavailable product states.
- Accessible quick actions and focus states.

## Out of Scope

- Full catalog search/filter/facet backend overhaul.
- Seller product management cards.
- Admin product cards.
- New merchandising campaign system.

## Constraints

- Product listing behavior must remain compatible with existing catalog APIs.
- Client-only filter limitations should not be expanded in this milestone unless needed for UI correctness.
- Cards must not create layout shift when images, badges, or quick actions load.
