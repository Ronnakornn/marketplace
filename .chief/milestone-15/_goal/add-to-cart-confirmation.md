# Add To Cart Confirmation

## Goal

Show buyers an immediate, understandable confirmation after add-to-cart so they know what happened and what to do next.

## In Scope

- Lightweight toast/action-bar confirmation pattern.
- Clear success copy.
- Actions to continue shopping and view cart.
- Optional buy-now handoff that routes to cart after successful add-to-cart.
- Product/variant context when available without exposing sensitive data.

## Out of Scope

- Mini cart drawer with item totals.
- Cross-seller cart summary.
- Checkout price recalculation UI.
- Cart promotion/coupon messaging.

## Constraints

- Confirmation must work on mobile and desktop.
- Confirmation must not block current product/listing interaction.
- Copy must remain readable in Thai and English contexts.
