# Goal: Product Media Workflow

## Outcome

Sellers and admins can manage product images and video through a complete media workflow.

## Scope

- Reuse the existing upload module for product image and video uploads.
- Support attaching completed uploads to products.
- Support image ordering, one primary image, alt text, and image removal.
- Support one product video.
- Keep existing upload content type and size validation.

## Success Criteria

- Publish readiness requires a primary product image.
- Product image limit remains enforced.
- Video upload rejects unsupported content types and oversized files.
- Seller/admin UI exposes media upload, reorder, primary selection, and validation states.
