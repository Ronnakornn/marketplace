# Product Detail Trust Content Scan

## Goal

Improve buyer product detail trust and content scanning so buyers can quickly understand product credibility, policies, and supporting detail before purchase.

## In Scope

- Frontend-only UX/UI polish using existing product detail, shop, review, Q&A, and discovery data.
- Shop trust section readability.
- Product facts, description, shipping, returns, and buyer protection scanability.
- Review and Q&A section layout polish.
- Related and recently viewed product discovery polish.

## Out of Scope

- Backend/API/schema changes.
- Checkout, payment, order, and fulfillment changes.
- Product listing/search/home redesign.
- Seller/admin product management surfaces.
- Purchase decision panel and sticky buy bar redesign beyond avoiding visual conflicts.

## Constraints

- Keep code in `app/features/product/` where practical.
- Reuse existing normalized product data and buyer components.
- Do not add fake dynamic trust metrics.
- Keep milestone PR-sized and incremental.
