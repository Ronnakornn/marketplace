# Product Detail Trust Content UI Contract

## Purpose

Product detail trust/content sections must help buyers evaluate the product and seller quickly using existing data.

## Content Contract

- Media/gallery section must clearly handle products with images, video, or no media.
- Shop trust section must show seller/shop identity, location where available, and buyer actions without crowding content.
- Policy and assurance copy must stay truthful and static when no backend data exists.
- Product facts must show condition, origin, warranty, brand, and attributes only when available.
- Description must support long text, line breaks, and missing description states.

## Layout Contract

- Keep the stable decision panel and sticky buy bar from milestone 17.
- Trust/content sections may be polished in place but must not reorder the entire page architecture.
- Sections must be scan-friendly on mobile and desktop.
- Long labels, long values, empty states, and action labels must not overlap or clip.

## Boundaries

- Do not add backend/API/schema fields.
- Do not invent dynamic shop metrics.
- Do not redesign listing/search/home surfaces.
- Do not change checkout, payment, or order behavior.
