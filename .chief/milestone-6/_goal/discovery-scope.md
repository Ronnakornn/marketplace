# Goal: Product Discovery and Merchandising Scope

## Outcome

Buyers can discover products through real marketplace data across homepage, category, search, recommendations, and merchandising sections.

## Scope

- Build pragmatic API and UX/UI support for discovery surfaces.
- Reuse catalog, search, recommendation, promotion, analytics, and marketing models where possible.
- Keep milestone 6 focused on product discovery and merchandising.
- Do not build a new ranking engine, AI search system, CMS, A/B testing platform, analytics dashboard, or full SEO program.

## Success Criteria

- Discovery screens consume real APIs instead of mock-only data blocks.
- Existing product detail and product management flows remain compatible.
- Discovery behavior is measurable through lightweight tracking events.
- Technical SEO baseline exists for product and category discovery pages.
