# Goal: Product Card Merchandising

## Outcome

Product cards communicate enough commercial information for buyers to compare products and take safe quick actions.

## Scope

- Primary image.
- Two-line title.
- Price or price range.
- Discount or original price when promotion data exists.
- Rating and sold count.
- Shop name and location.
- Badges for flash sale, free shipping, verified/preferred shop, low stock, and new when data exists.
- Favorite quick action.
- Conditional quick add-to-cart only when no variant selection is required.
- Impression and click tracking.
- Do not implement comparison mode or full quick-view modal.

## Success Criteria

- Product cards remain stable across mobile and desktop grids.
- Quick add-to-cart never adds an ambiguous variant.
- Missing optional merchandising data degrades gracefully.
- Product clicks and impressions can be tracked.
