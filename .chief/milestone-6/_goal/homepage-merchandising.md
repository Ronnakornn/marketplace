# Goal: Homepage Merchandising

## Outcome

The marketplace homepage presents buyer-relevant product discovery sections backed by real data.

## Scope

- Banner carousel.
- Category grid or tree.
- Flash sale rail.
- Recommended products.
- New arrivals.
- Top or featured shops.
- Recently viewed products when user or session signal exists.
- Voucher or promotion strip when APIs are ready.
- Do not implement a full CMS or drag-and-drop layout builder.

## Success Criteria

- Homepage sections handle loading, empty, error, and partial data states.
- Section ordering is stable and predictable.
- Sections link to category, product, shop, promotion, or search destinations.
- Missing optional data does not break the homepage.
