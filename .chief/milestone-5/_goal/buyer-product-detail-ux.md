# Goal: Buyer Product Detail UX

## Outcome

Buyer product detail supports SKU-aware purchase decisions and clear product inspection without a full storefront redesign.

## Scope

- Add interactive image gallery and video slot.
- Add option-based variant picker.
- Disable out-of-stock option values.
- Add quantity stepper constrained by selected variant stock.
- Update price and stock display based on selected variant.
- Require selected variant before add-to-cart or buy-now.
- Show category specs, shop card, chat/follow actions, and review preview.
- Do not redesign homepage or full search layout.

## Success Criteria

- Buyer cannot add to cart without a valid selected variant.
- Buyer cannot select quantity above available stock.
- Sticky CTA remains usable on mobile and desktop.
- Product detail handles unavailable product, no media, no variants, and out-of-stock states.
