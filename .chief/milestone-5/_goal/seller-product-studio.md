# Goal: Seller Product Studio

## Outcome

Sellers can create, edit, validate, and submit products for review from a single editor experience.

## Scope

- Use a single product editor page with section navigation.
- Sections: Basics, Category & Specs, Media, Variants, Inventory, Review.
- Show product status, readiness checklist, save state, and moderation result.
- Support save draft and submit for review.
- Do not use a wizard-only flow.

## Success Criteria

- Seller product list supports search, status filtering, and empty/loading/error states.
- Product editor supports draft edits across all required sections.
- Submit review is blocked until readiness requirements are met.
- Rejection reason and moderation status are visible to the seller.
